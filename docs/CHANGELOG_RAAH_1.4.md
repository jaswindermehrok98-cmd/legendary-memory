# Changelog — RAAH Personal Edition 1.4

## Cinematic visual pass
- Added a camera-following procedural sky dome with layered sky colour, soft cloud banks, and a warm horizon highlight.
- Enabled filmic tone mapping and sRGB gamma correction on the camera when available in PlayCanvas 2.23.2.
- Rebalanced late-day ambient and directional light.
- Extended linear fog distances so the district fades more gradually at the horizon.
- Increased directional shadow resolution from 512 to 1024.
- Enabled shadow casting on district building volumes and selected human body meshes.
- Added warm practical lights at the chai stall, a market shopfront, and the weighbridge booth.
- Added broken lane dashes, subtle asphalt repair patches, and stylised zebra crossings at city intersections.
- Raised the Balanced default render scale and added a native-resolution Sharper preset.
- Enabled antialiasing on the graphics device and added a restrained cinematic grade overlay.

## Preserved
- Six-settlement district coordinates and construction.
- Existing taxis, enterable portals, interior rooms, story characters, conversations and personal story.
- Case 001–003 evidence, interviews, theories, journal, map, and local save systems.
- PlayCanvas 2.23.2 CDN architecture.

## Limitations
- Stylised procedural assets remain; this is not photorealistic AAA.
- Taxis remain fast travel; vehicles are not player-drivable.
- Interiors remain portal transitions.
- Live browser rendering and integrated-laptop FPS require validation.
